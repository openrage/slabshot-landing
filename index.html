What it does

Auto-logged catches

One tap marks a fish. The app stamps the spot, depth, temp and time — and groups your photos to it afterward. No forms in the boat.

Fish-finder sync

Connects to your Lowrance/Garmin/Humminbird over Wi-Fi for the live sonar track and depth. No unit? Bank mode logs from your phone's GPS.

Private by design

Your spots stay yours. Share cards blur location to a county area and carry no GPS. Everything works offline — nothing's required to leave the boat.

Read the day

Live weather, barometric trend, sunrise/sunset and moon — plus your own log-wide insights once you've fished a few trips.
